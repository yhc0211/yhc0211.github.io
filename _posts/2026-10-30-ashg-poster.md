I presented our work, **"Isoform-level Expression Improves Survival Signal Selection and Prediction,"** at the **ASHG 2026 Annual Meeting** in Montréal, Canada.

We developed a penalized regression framework to investigate whether isoform-level expression improves survival-associated feature selection and restricted mean survival time (RMST) prediction compared with gene-level expression.

Using simulations based on TCGA breast cancer expression data, we found that isoform-level models improved feature selection and survival prediction, particularly when isoforms from the same gene had opposing effects.
